01_SOURCE_IDENTITY: issuer,document no,type,publication/effective dates,version,territorial scope,limits. publication!=application. EEA relevance!=automatic EEA applicability.
02_ACTOR_DUTY: actor,right/interest,duty/safeguard,condition,locator,exception,remedy/enforcement,binding,limits. principle/recommendation!=mandatory duty.
03_HSA_THREATS: threat_id,name,relation,summary,locator,limits. Taxonomy seçili değilse THREAT_ID=UNKNOWN; textual threat yaz. relation=DIRECT_EXPLICIT|ANALOGICAL|CONTEXT_ONLY|NO_EXPLICIT_SUPPORT_FOUND.
04_EVIDENCE: duty/question,evidence type/holder,SOURCE_REQUIRED|RESEARCH_USEFUL,integrity,locator,limits. Kaynak açıkça istemiyorsa engineering best-practice'i SOURCE_REQUIRED yapma.
05_UNKNOWN_CONFLICT: KNOWN|UNKNOWN|CONFLICT|CURRENTNESS_LIMIT|TRANSLATION_LIMIT|SCOPE_LIMIT|EVIDENCE_GAP + description/materiality/locator/resolution/next research.
06_ENGINEERING: duty/safeguard,control candidate,test candidate,evidence artifact,destination,reviewer,promotion,limits. Control/test ID üretme; implemented/tested/effective deme.
07_LOCATOR_INDEX: claim summary,source_id,exact locator,type,limits. Her material claim locator içersin.
