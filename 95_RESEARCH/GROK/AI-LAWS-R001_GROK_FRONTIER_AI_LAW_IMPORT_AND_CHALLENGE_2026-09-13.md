### 3.2 EU Product Liability Directive currentness

Directive (EU) 2024/2853 expressly brings software, including AI systems, into the product-liability framework. However, Member States have until **9 December 2026** to transpose it. At the current date, 13 September 2026, it must not be described as if a uniform new national PLD regime were already fully implemented across the EU.

**Disposition:**


### 3.3 UNESCO Neurotechnology authority class

### 3.4 Chile neurorights scope

Chile provides a genuine constitutional/legal neurorights research anchor and the 2023 `Girardi v. Emotiv` litigation is a concrete neurotechnology/data case. However:

- Chile-specific constitutional protection is not a universal global rule;
- the implementation bill `Boletín 13.828-19` remains in legislative processing in 2026;
- coverage of generic LLM-inferred mental/emotional states cannot be inferred automatically from EEG/neurotechnology cases.

**Disposition:** current-law core supported; generic AI-inference extension remains an open comparative-law question.

### 3.10 National-security/state-system exclusions

The EU AI Act contains exclusions for systems used exclusively for military, defence or national-security purposes. This supports a real accountability-gap research question.

It does **not** establish that state systems are wholly unregulated or that all closed government AI systems are outside law. Constitutional, administrative, intelligence-oversight, procurement, criminal, human-rights and international-law regimes must be mapped separately.

## 6. Responsibility architecture extracted from the input

The Grok actor table is useful as a research decomposition, not a global liability rule. AI-LAWS should test at least:

- model developer / GPAI provider;

For every actor the legal chain remains jurisdiction-specific:

```text
ACTOR_ROLE
-> DUTY_SOURCE
-> FACTUAL_CONDUCT
-> BREACH_STANDARD
-> FORESEEABILITY / KNOWLEDGE WHERE RELEVANT
-> CAUSATION
-> LEGALLY_RECOGNISED_DAMAGE
-> DEFENCE / IMMUNITY / CONTRACT
-> REMEDY
```

No step may be inferred from the existence of another.
